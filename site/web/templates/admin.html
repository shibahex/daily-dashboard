Model servers on your network

Besides a model on their own computer, people's browsers may talk to these (Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp or another OpenAI-style server) for the Local model AI. Their chats and notes go straight there, never through this server. One address per line, like https://models.example.lan; nothing else on your network is reachable.

Browsers check HTTPS certificates themselves and no page can skip that: with a self-signed or expired one, each person opens the address once and accepts the warning, or adds your certificate authority to their browser.
