MyFitnessPal doesn't let other apps sign in with your password, so you copy your login from your browser instead. Do this on a computer, not a phone.

  1. Log in to MyFitnessPal in a normal window (not private or incognito). Open MyFitnessPal ↗
  2. Open the developer tools on that tab: press F12 (Mac: ⌘ ⌥ I) and click the Application tab (Firefox and Safari call it Storage; it may hide behind »).
  3. Copy your login: open Cookies → https://www.myfitnesspal.com, type remember_me in the filter box, then double-click its Value and copy it (Ctrl C, Mac: ⌘ C).
  4. Paste it below and press Connect.
Help for your browser
  • Safari: first turn on the developer tools: Safari → Settings → Advanced → Show features for web developers. Then Develop → Show Web Inspector → Storage → Cookies.
  • No remember_me? Log out of MyFitnessPal and log in again in a normal window, then reload the cookie list.
  • Another way: in the Network tab, reload the page, right-click the first line and choose Copy → Copy as cURL (Firefox: Copy Value → Copy as cURL). Pasting that works too.
