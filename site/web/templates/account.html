Your other data on the server's disk

{{if .Encrypted}}

Your plan, checklist, weight, tasks, time and food are encrypted on the server's disk. The server opens them while you're signed in (it builds your pages from them) and locks them when you log out, after 30 idle minutes, and when it stops; meanwhile your MyFitnessPal sync waits.

On a server whose disk is already encrypted and that only you use, this adds little, and losing both your password and recovery code would lose this data. You can turn it off; your notes stay encrypted either way.

{{else}}

Your plan, checklist, weight, tasks, time and food are kept as plain files on the server's disk{{if .PlainStorage}}, as you chose{{end}}. Anyone who can read the server's files can read them. Your notes, chat and keys are encrypted either way.

You'll get a new recovery code, for this data and your notes.

{{end}}
